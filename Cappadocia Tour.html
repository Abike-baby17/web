Cappadocia is often referred to as the "open-air film studio." George Lucas himself was stunned by the local landscapes and immortalized them in Star Wars. Locals claim that they often see UFOs in the skies. Also, in Goreme, in one of the caves, there is a real UFO museum, the only one in Asia Minor.

In Cappadocia, an annual classical music festival is held, which is organized in the caves. Thousands of tourists gather to enjoy this unusual concert. The region has an abundance of vineyards. Are you already interested? Welcome to Cappadocia, then!

Fascinating Cappadpocia attracts tourists from all over the world with its intricate landscapes created by the volcanic nature of the area, cave temples and settlements, and the Goreme National Natural Park.

Excursions to Cappadocia provide an opportunity to see underground tunnels and unearthly landscapes, spend the night in a cave (in the so-called cave hotel),where comfortable hotels are located, and look at the stunning landscape from a balloon, breathe in crystal clear transparent air and visit underground monasteries.

Hot air balloon rides are the highlight of Cappadocia. They allow you to see all the beauties and features of the region from a bird's eye view. Tens of thousands of tourists come here every year for this entertainment.

The main feature of Cappadocia is its landscapes of volcanic origin, as well as man-made caves, villages, and even entire underground cities. The first such dwellings were built long before our era and were a refuge for Christians fleeing the persecution of the Roman Empire.

Tufa formations shaped like stone chimneys or rock mushrooms have become a mainstay of tourism in the town of Zelve. Local legends say that fairies live in bizarre rocks, giving people prophetic dreams.
