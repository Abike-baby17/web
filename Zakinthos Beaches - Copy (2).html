Zakinthos Beaches

Zakinthos, also known as Zante, boasts a variety of stunning beaches, each offering a unique experience.

Some of the most popular include Navagio (Shipwreck Beach), known for its iconic wreck and dramatic cliffs, and Gerakas,
a protected beach known for sea turtle nesting. Zakinthos Beach is a must-visit paradise for beach lovers and adventure seekers.

Other favourites include Porto Limnionas, a snorkeling paradise, and Tsilivi, a long sandy beach with plenty of amenities.

Zakinthos's best beaches

For Dramatic Scenery:

  • Navagio (Shipwreck Beach)

This world-famous beach is accessible only by boat and features a rusty shipwreck
resting on its sandy shore, surrounded by towering cliffs.

  • Porto Limnionas

This beach is a haven for snorkeling and diving enthusiasts, with its crystal-clear
waters and rocky coastline.

For Families and Relaxation:

  • Gerakas

This protected beach is a nesting ground for sea turtles, making it a popular spot
for nature lovers.

  • Banana Beach

A long, sandy beach with shallow waters, ideal for families with young children.

  • Tsilivi

A long, sandy beach with plenty of amenities, including watersports, restuarants, bars.

  • Laganas

A popular beach for nightlife and watersports, also known for its sea turtle nesting,
